# Daya Medika Pratama

> PT Daya Medika Pratama (DMP) is an Indonesian healthcare IT company in Jakarta that
> builds and implements hospital information systems and insurance core systems. Active
> in IT since 2007 — first as a systems integrator building data-acquisition systems for
> textile production machinery, later as a Microsoft Business Solution Partner in
> healthcare and education — it now sells three things: AFYA Better HIS (a hospital
> information system with an openEHR-based clinical data repository, in use at 40+
> Indonesian hospitals), AFYA Insurance (an insurance policy and claims management
> system), and implementation of the Better digital health platform and Microsoft
> Dynamics 365. Its positioning line is "One Patient One Record for Better Health".

## Important for agents: there is no Daya Medika Pratama API

Daya Medika Pratama publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no SDK, no webhooks
and no machine-readable contract**. As of 2026-09-02 there is nothing here for an agent
to call. Specifically:

- No `api.`, `developer.`, `docs.` or `portal.` subdomain of dayamedikapratama.com
  resolves in DNS.
- `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/v1/openapi.json`, `/api-docs`,
  `/graphql`, `/mcp`, `/llms.txt` and `/apis.json` all return HTTP 404 on both
  dayamedikapratama.com and afyabetter.com.
- Every `/.well-known/` path probed (security.txt, openid-configuration,
  oauth-authorization-server, api-catalog, ai-plugin.json, agent-card.json, agent.json)
  returns HTTP 404 on both hosts.
- There is no GitHub organization for the company and no first-party package on npm,
  PyPI, Maven Central, NuGet, RubyGems, crates.io or pkg.go.dev.

The standards DMP names — openEHR, HL7 FHIR, SATUSEHAT (the Indonesian Ministry of
Health's national health data exchange) and BPJS Kesehatan (the national health
insurance payer) — are APIs the company's products **consume** on behalf of a hospital
customer. They are not APIs DMP publishes. The one documented bulk-data path into AFYA
Better HIS is an Excel template import performed by a hospital administrator inside the
application, not an API.

If you need to integrate with a DMP deployment, the entry point is a sales conversation:
https://dayamedikapratama.com/contact-us/

## Solutions

- [AFYA Better HIS](https://dayamedikapratama.com/solutions/afya-better-his/): hospital
  information system covering registration, electronic medical records, pharmacy,
  laboratory, appointments and BPJS claims; stated as connected to BPJS, Kemenkes
  (SATUSEHAT), FHIR and Better. Product site: https://afyabetter.com/
- [AFYA Insurance](https://dayamedikapratama.com/solutions/afya-insurance/): policy
  management, claims processing, customer portal, analytics and reporting for insurers,
  brokers and policyholders.
- [Better Platform](https://dayamedikapratama.com/solutions/better-platform/): DMP is a
  partner of Better (better.care) for the openEHR-based digital health platform in the
  Indonesian market; the partnership extends DMP's HIS into the clinical domain with a
  clinical data repository and low-code tools.
- [Dynamics 365](https://dayamedikapratama.com/solutions/dynamics-365/): Microsoft
  Dynamics 365 ERP/CRM, positioned as hospital back office.

## Product documentation
